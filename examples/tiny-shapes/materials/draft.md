All settings and numbers in this demo are synthetic. The paragraphs below intentionally contain claims to check; they are not validated scientific statements.

## Abstract

Our augmentation consistently improves classification under every evaluated condition and establishes broad robustness across distribution shifts.

## Methods

We used a convolutional classifier. Models were trained for 20 epochs and the best model was selected. We report mean accuracy over five seeds. Both methods use the same classifier and Augmented adds training noise.

## Results

The augmentation outperformed the baseline in every condition. These results demonstrate that the model learns invariant representations.
